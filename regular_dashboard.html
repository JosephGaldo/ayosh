AYOSH

Automated Year-round Occupational Safety and Health

AYOSH is a centralized compliance platform that enables branches to upload OSH requirements into a standardized system, automatically categorizes submissions, tracks compliance in real-time via AI-powered dashboards (leveraging Secure GPT), and generates Executive-ready reports in minutes. The system includes auto-notifications to keep branches on track and provides Corp Sec with actionable insights for strategic decision-making.
